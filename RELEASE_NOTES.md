# ✨ Evexia V1.0 — Release Notes

> 📚 A calmer way to plan, study, and keep life together.

Evexia V1.0 is the first release of Evexia, bringing your schedule, tasks, study materials, documents, and optional AI study help into one place.

## 🌟 What's included

- 🏠 **Home dashboard** — get an overview of your day, upcoming tasks, and recent files.
- 📅 **Subjects and timetable** — organise subjects and build a weekly schedule with repeating classes, locations, and colour-coded subjects.
- ✅ **Tasks and reminders** — manage deadlines, priorities, repeat rules, and optional notifications.
- 📝 **Notes and flashcards** — keep study notes and revision cards organised by subject.
- 📂 **Library** — import documents, save links, and access recent or bookmarked items.
- 📄 **PDF tools** — scan pages, create and combine PDFs, convert pages to images, and print documents.
- 🤖 **Evexia AI** — ask questions and get summaries of notes or documents through Puter.
- 🎨 **Personalisation** — choose a theme and accent colour, adjust motion, and enable optional home-screen widgets.
- 🪪 **Student ID** — keep student ID information within the app.
- 📱 **Android support** — designed for Android 8.0 and newer.

## 🔐 Privacy and permissions

- Your subjects, timetable, tasks, notes, and study content are stored on your device.
- No Evexia account is required for the main planning and study features.
- Evexia has no ads or analytics.
- **Evexia AI is optional and online.** Prompts, conversation context, and text extracted from documents you submit are sent to Puter and the selected AI provider. AI chat history is stored locally. Don't submit information you want to keep private.
- Notification permission is used for task reminders. Home-screen widgets are optional.
- Android's device-backup settings may include the app's local data.

## 🚀 Getting started

1. Install Evexia on a device running Android 8.0 or newer.
2. Add your subjects and timetable.
3. Start creating tasks, notes, and flashcards, or organise your documents in the Library.
4. Enable reminders and widgets as needed.
5. Sign in to a Puter account and connect to the internet only if you want to use Evexia AI.
